Criminal Law

Your freedom matters most.

Have you been arrested or charged with a crime? When your liberty is at stake, you want to make sure you have experience on your side. Crest Law has experience in all stages of the criminal process, from inception through appeals and post-conviction. With experience in both State and Federal criminal matters, Crest Law is here to guide you through this trying time with the compassion and comittment you deserve. We will fight for your liberty, because we would want someone to fight for ours.

It is ultimately your decision; however, even if you have been falsely accused of a crime, you will likely benefit from representation. Your liberty is no laughing matter and it is worth making sure it remains yours.

The cost of representation varies based upon the significance of the charges and other factors. Contact us today for a free, no-obligation consultation.

Unfortunately, we cannot state with certainty how long any individual matter will take. Crest Law will treat your matter with the care, respect, and efficiency that you deserve.

We would love to represent you, but we want to learn more about your particular matter first. Simply click the contact us button to inquire confidentialy. An attorney will contact you within 24 hours.
