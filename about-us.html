ABOUT US

Njura Karimi & Company Advocates is a Kenya-based legal practice focused on providing considered legal support and practical guidance. We work to help clients understand their rights, obligations and options, and to make informed decisions at important moments. Our approach is built around preparation, professional integrity, clear communication and respect for each client’s circumstances. We seek to understand the facts, identify the legal issues, explain realistic pathways and keep clients informed about agreed next steps.
